Competition + Fun Oil Discovery Model

Human and AI players use the same rules. The fun comes from racing for land, reading clues, taking drilling risks, building connected production networks, and out-scaling rivals.

Core Multiplayer Rule
Human Player Research, buy land, assign manpower, acquire equipment, build routes, drill, produce.
same rules
AI Company Uses the same actions and objects, but chooses by strategy personality.

No separate AI-only magic. AI wins by the same things the player does: reserves, production, cash, technology, routes, and facility capacity.

How Players Compete
Land Race First player to buy a block controls its wells, routes, and facility placement.
Information Race Survey and seismic clues are private until a player reveals or drills the area.
Route Race Roads and pipelines need connected tiles. Rival land can force longer routes.
Capacity Race Rivals who build gathering/tank capacity faster can monetize discoveries earlier.
Market Race Early producers sell at better prices; oversupply can reduce price for everyone.
Tech Race Better tech improves discovery odds, drilling cost, production rate, and environment control.
Environmental Pressure Overbuilding causes taxes, cleanup costs, shutdown events, or land-buy restrictions.
Victory Race Players compete on cash, company value, production, reserves, or technology.
Turn Flow For Human Or AI Matches
1. Plan Choose research, buy land, assign crews, queue builds, pick drill targets.
2. Resolve Actions spend turns, routes build tile by tile, wells drill, production flows.
3. Reveal New clues, drilling results, rival discoveries, events, and market changes appear.
4. Score Cash, reserves, production, tech, environment, and company value update.

For solo: player turn then AI turn. For online later: simultaneous planning, then one shared resolution step.

AI Company Personalities
AI Type What It Prioritizes How It Pressures Player
Explorer buys wide land, surveys many blocks, drills many cheap wells finds discoveries before the player and reveals hot zones
Infrastructure Builder roads, pipelines, gathering centers, tank farms controls efficient routes and scales production faster
Tech Investor research, expert manpower, better equipment starts slower but outperforms in mid and late game
Fast Producer turning small discoveries into cash quickly wins early market money and forces the player to keep up
How Finding Oil Becomes Fun
Hidden Basin oil potential exists but is unknown
Surface Clue survey finds seep, structure, rock clue, or nothing
Seismic Clue confidence ring shows low / medium / high prospect
Drill Decision player sees risk, cost, chance, and possible prize
Reveal Result dry hole, oil show, gas, discovery, or giant field

Why This Is Interesting

  • You can see a good prospect, but the AI may own the easiest route.
  • You can buy around the rival, but that costs more turns and cash.
  • You can drill early with weak evidence, or wait for better seismic and risk losing land.
  • You can build capacity first, or chase another discovery.

Vehicle & Equipment Tree + Activity Objects

Equipment progression unlocks machines and field packages. Activities stay separate and produce map objects such as wells, roads, pipelines, and gathering centers.

Balance Assumption
Costs Game cash in millions. Example: $8M means 8 million in game money.
Turns Standard game speed. These are acquisition/build prep turns, not real calendar time.
Rule Equipment nodes are assets. Activities like drilling or building pipeline are not equipment nodes.

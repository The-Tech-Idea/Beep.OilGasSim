Facility Objects: Size, Capacity, Upgrades

Gathering centers and tank farms are map objects with size and capacity. The player places them, connects them with pipelines, then upgrades capacity as production grows.

Corrected Facility Rule

Wells Produce Flow

Each producing well creates oil/gas units per turn.

Pipelines Move Flow

Connected pipeline segments have route throughput.

Gathering Centers Process Flow

They accept connected wells until capacity is full.

Tank Farms Store Overflow

They hold produced oil when sales/export capacity is constrained.

Simple Strategic Consequences
Underbuilt Gathering Wells are choked, so revenue is lower until capacity is upgraded.
No Tank Storage Market/export disruption wastes production or forces shut-in turns.
Oversized Facilities High upfront cost and upkeep slow expansion if built too early.
Good Network Planning Higher production score, fewer bottlenecks, better late-game growth.

Money Requires A Complete Chain

  1. Well produces flow.
  2. Pipeline route connects well to gathering center.
  3. Gathering center has enough throughput.
  4. Tank farm buffers overflow or export delay.
  5. Export/sales converts production into cash per turn.
Well flow: 24 / turn Gathering capacity: 40 / turn Tank storage: 120 Revenue status: Connected
Cash Formula
cash per turn = sold production x market price - staff upkeep - equipment upkeep - facility upkeep - environment tax / penalty
Example24 flow sold x $2M price = $48M gross
Costs$5M staff + $4M equipment + $3M facilities + $2M environment
Net$34M cash this turn
Bottlenecks Make It A Game
No Tech Action is locked until researched.
No Manpower Action is available but cannot be staffed.
No Equipment Action cannot start until asset is acquired.
No Route Well exists but production cannot reach a hub.
Low Capacity Gathering center chokes wells.
No Storage Production is wasted or shut in during market/export events.
High Environment Pressure More tax, cleanup cost, or temporary land-buy ban.
Rival Expansion Opponent buys nearby blocks and blocks better routes.
Game Objectives
Economic Victory Reach $1B company value or highest cash score after final turn.
Production Victory Connect and sustain 300 flow / turn across your fields.
Reserve Victory Discover the largest proven reserve base through exploration.
Technology Victory Complete late-game recovery and automation techs first.

Available Actions

After Purchase

  1. Send Geologist to map surface clues.
  2. Run seismic to reveal prospect confidence.
  3. Build road and pad with crews.
  4. Send rig crew to drill.
Environment As Civ-Style Pressure
What Adds Pressure Roads, pads, wells, flaring, water use, spills, overbuilding.
What Reduces Pressure Waste handling, cleaner power, monitoring, remediation crews, better tech.
Budget Consequence Carbon tax, cleanup costs, insurance, operating surcharge per turn.
Strategic Punishment Fine, forced shutdown, reputation loss, or land-buy moratorium for 2-5 turns.
Event Example

Environmental load is too high near a sensitive basin. Pay $18M cleanup now, or accept a 3-turn ban on buying new land plus higher operating tax.

How This Feels Like AoE + Civilization

AoE Feel

  • Race rivals for valuable land blocks.
  • Send crews across the desert map.
  • Build camps, roads, rig yards, pipelines, and defenses.
  • Opponent pressure comes from expansion timing and infrastructure control.

Civ Feel

  • Research choices unlock future strategy.
  • Environmental and economic consequences shape long-term planning.
  • Field actions can boost related research.
  • Players can win by production, reserves, market control, or technology lead.

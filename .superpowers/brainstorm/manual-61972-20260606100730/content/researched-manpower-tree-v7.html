Research-Backed Manpower Tree

A simple Civ-style workforce tree for playable crews. It unlocks people you send on the map, not HR systems, contracts, or paperwork.

Research Takeaway

Age Of Empires Pattern

Workers gather/build, scouts reveal the map, production buildings train units, and new ages/buildings unlock stronger options.

Civilization Pattern

Long-term progression should be readable as a prerequisite tree. Units and specialists matter because they change what actions the player can take.

Oil & Gas Reality

Exploration starts with geology and seismic work, then moves through drilling, completions, production, and reservoir management.

How A Crew Node Works
Prerequisite Parent crew node plus the right facility.
Training Cost Cash + manpower capacity + 2-5 turns.
Map Action Every crew unlocks a simple thing to do on a tile.
Upgrade Effect Later nodes improve speed, range, risk, or yield.
Example Action Requirements

Run 2D Seismic

Needs: owned land, 2D Seismic tech, Seismic Crew, $8M.

Result: reveals prospect confidence and boosts Seismic Processing research.

Drill Wildcat Well

Needs: prospect, Exploration Wells tech, Rig Crew, road or pad, $35M.

Result: dry hole, discovery, or commercial field.

Start Production

Needs: successful well, Completion Crew, Production Operators, infrastructure.

Result: revenue per turn plus environmental pressure.

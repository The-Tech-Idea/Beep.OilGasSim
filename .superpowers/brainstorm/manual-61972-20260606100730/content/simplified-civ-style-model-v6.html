Simplified Civ-Style Oil Strategy Model

Keep the actual game simple: research capabilities, buy land, send crews, build infrastructure, drill, produce, and handle consequences. Business terms can be names, not separate gameplay systems.

What The Player Actually Does
1. Research Choose a tech. It completes after turns based on science/data output.
2. Buy Land Click a block, pay cash, own it. No permit minigame.
3. Send Crew Geologist, seismic crew, road crew, rig crew, production crew.
4. Build / Drill Roads, camp, pad, well, pipeline, processing plant.
5. Produce Earn revenue, increase reserves score, fund expansion.
6. Handle Pressure Environment, upkeep, rival expansion, market swings.
What Is Not Active Gameplay

Removed As Separate Systems

  • No contract negotiation screen.
  • No pipeline access paperwork mechanic.
  • No JV deal management loop.
  • No service-contract administration.

Allowed As Names / Flavor

  • A tech can be called Pipeline Integration.
  • A production upgrade can be called Service Fleet.
  • A score bonus can mention export contracts.
  • A map objective can be named Regional Offtake.

Rule: if it does not create a simple map action, a research choice, or a consequence, it stays as naming flavor and does not become another system.

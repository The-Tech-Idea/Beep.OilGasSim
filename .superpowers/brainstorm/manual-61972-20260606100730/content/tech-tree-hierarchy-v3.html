Revised Again: Pure Technical Research Tree

Not In Tech Tree

These are gameplay systems, but not research:

  • Land permits
  • License auctions
  • Environmental approvals
  • Joint venture contracts
  • Market deals and financing

Where Permits Go

Permits become operational gates. Example: you may know “Exploration Wells,” but you still need license rights and field access before sending a rig.

Game Loop

  1. Research technical capability.
  2. Acquire license/permit/access separately.
  3. Assign crew and resources.
  4. Watch the crew perform the action on the map.

Fun Action Simulation

1. Research Turn

Pick one tech. It consumes turns, cash, and specialist capacity.

2. Assign Crew

Send Geologists, Survey Crew, Rig Crew, Engineers, or Construction Crew to map tiles.

3. Watch Map Movement

Crews travel by desert tracks, make camps, run surveys, drill wells, and build pipelines.

4. Compete With Humans

Human companies race for licenses, research unlocks, crews, discoveries, fields, and export routes.
